The Arcon Park Community Run

A loop through our own streets — Gabro, Zircon, Ironstone, Malachite — ending back at the gym. Every neighbour, every pace, welcome.

Distance
≈ 4.2 km loop
Start / Finish
Arcon Park Gym
When
1st Saturday, 7:00 AM
Who
All ages, run or walk

The route

Start at Arcon Park Gym on Andesite Ave. Head up Gabro Ave to Beril Rd, then follow Beril Rd as it curves along Zircon Ave and down past Sandstone Ave. Loop back down through Tilliet Ave and Malachite Ave, cutting across on Asur Ave and back down Gabro Ave to Andesite Ave and the finish.

Distance is an estimate from the mapped route — worth confirming with a GPS watch or app on the first walk-through before it's printed anywhere official.

Why we're running

This isn't just exercise — it's the first regular reason for the whole street to show up in one place. Every run is a chance to talk about what we're building: local jobs, community-owned businesses, and a media centre we can be proud of.

Organised by the Waldrift Arcon Park community movement. Bring water, wear something reflective if it's still dark, and bring a neighbour who hasn't come before.
